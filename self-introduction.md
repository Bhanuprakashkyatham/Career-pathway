## Tips for Delivery
1. **Speak confidently** - maintain eye contact (or camera contact in virtual interviews)
2. **Pace yourself** - don't rush, take natural pauses
3. **Smile** - be enthusiastic but professional
4. **Customize** - adapt based on the job description
5. **Practice** - rehearse 10 times before interviews

## Common Follow-up Questions to Prepare
1. "Tell me about your current project"
2. "What's your biggest achievement?"
3. "Why are you looking for a change?"
4. "What are your strengths and weaknesses?"
5. "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"

---
*Practice this daily until it becomes natural!*
